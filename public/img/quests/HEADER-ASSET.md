# Illustration Quêtes et récompenses

`quests-rewards.webp` : image transparente de 256 × 256 px, générée avec l’outil intégré imagegen, optimisée avec sharp en WebP qualité 85, alpha qualité 100.

Utilisée dans l’en-tête de `src/views/rewards.ejs`, dans le même cadre que les illustrations de bâtiments.

## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent header illustration for quests and rewards in a medieval strategy browser game. Primary request: one compact open medieval wooden treasure chest with antique bronze fittings, a few gold coins inside, and a rolled ivory parchment quest scroll with a burgundy wax seal leaning diagonally against the chest. Style: detailed hand-painted medieval game inventory art, slightly isometric three-quarter perspective, warm antique gold and bronze, parchment cream, weathered brown wood, restrained burgundy accents. Strong compact silhouette readable at 100 pixels, refined material detail at larger sizes. Centered isolated still life occupying 88 percent of square canvas; chest and scroll form one coherent icon. No surrounding scene, no pedestal, no UI frame, no text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no sparkle effects. True transparent background, no baked checkerboard, no background shadow.
