# Planche « Palais de glace »

Mode : imagegen intégré, fond transparent. La planche romaine sert de gabarit pour la perspective, les six positions et la progression des tailles.

Prompt final :

> Create six transparent upgrade-level map sprites for an ice palace fantasy village. Use the Roman village sheet as a strict 3-by-2 composition template: same compact footprints, elevated isometric 3/4 camera, roof orientations, centered front gate, bottom baseline, relative sizes, broad transparent gutters and readable simple game-icon silhouettes. Replace Roman architecture with sculpted crystalline ice: pale cyan walls, blue ice roofs, frosted foundations, cobalt shadows and small faceted accents. Level 1: two low ice cottages and frost fence. Level 2: two cottages in a blue-ice palisade. Level 3: two cottages, low ice wall and small pavilion. Level 4: four modest corner towers and two cottages. Level 5: medium two-tier ice hall and compact wall. Level 6: three-tier ice palace with four low cottages and centered gate. Keep forms broad and legible, without dense icicles, blizzard, giant spikes or Christmas decoration. Maintain contrast against green grass. No people, text, numbers, logos, flags, scenery or watermark. True transparent background.

La génération brute est `palais-glace-generation.png`. La planche normalisée mesure 1200 × 680. Découpe reproductible : `php scripts/process-palais-glace-sheet.php` depuis la racine du projet.
