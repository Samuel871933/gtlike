# Planche « Islam »

Générée avec l’outil image intégré, en prenant les planches Christianisme et Empire arabo-musulman comme références de style, de perspective et de dimensions.

> Create one coherent 3 columns by 2 rows sprite sheet of six distinct Islamic villages on a genuinely transparent background. Use crisp hand-painted isometric game art, front-facing gates, compact footprints, warm sandstone and white stucco, cobalt and turquoise glazed domes, pointed arches and geometric tile detail. Start with two houses, a small mosque and a slender minaret; progressively add homes, walls, courtyard buildings and larger domes; finish with a fortified town around a grand mosque with two minarets. Keep all six levels visually consistent and increasingly complex while readable at map-sprite size. Leave clear gutters between cells. No people, calligraphy, text, numbers, interface, watermark, extra ground plane or overlapping sprites.

La planche PNG transparente est découpée par `scripts/process-islam-sheet.php` en six fichiers `level-N.png` aux dimensions des autres designs. Les versions WebP accompagnent les PNG.
