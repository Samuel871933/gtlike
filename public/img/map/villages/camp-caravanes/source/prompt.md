# Planche « Camp de caravanes »

Mode : imagegen intégré, fond transparent. La planche romaine du projet sert de gabarit pour la disposition, la perspective et les tailles. Le script replace ensuite les silhouettes sur les mêmes centres et lignes de base.

Prompt final :

> Create a playful modern white-caravan camp game sprite sheet with six upgrade levels, 3 columns by 2 rows. Use the supplied Roman village sheet as a strict composition template: same shallow footprints, elevated isometric 3/4 direction, centered front entrance, vertical alignment, relative sizes, broad transparent gutters and compact readable silhouettes. Replace ancient buildings with neat modern white travel caravans and small camper trailers, gray windows and blue-gray trim. Humor comes only from a tiny campsite becoming a ridiculously organized caravan town. Level 1: two caravans and short fence. Level 2: two caravans, awning and entry opening. Level 3: three caravans and gate. Level 4: four caravans around an open court. Level 5: five caravans and a larger camper at the rear. Level 6: six caravans, a larger camper and neat perimeter. Keep forms simple and legible at 50 pixels. No people, ethnic symbols or caricature, text, logos, flags, trash or watermark. Genuine transparent background.

La génération brute est `camp-caravanes-generation.png`. La planche finale mesure 1200 × 680. Découpe reproductible : `php scripts/process-camp-caravanes-sheet.php` depuis la racine du projet.
