# Planche « Ville de l’enfer »

Mode : imagegen intégré, fond transparent. La planche romaine sert de gabarit pour la perspective, les six positions et la progression des tailles.

Prompt final :

> Create six transparent upgrade-level map sprites for a fantasy hell city. Use the Roman village sheet as a strict 3-by-2 composition template: same compact shallow footprints, elevated isometric 3/4 camera, roof orientations, centered front entrance, bottom baseline, relative sizes, broad transparent gutters and simple readable game-icon silhouettes. Replace Roman buildings with dark charcoal obsidian, ash-gray stone, restrained orange lava seams and controlled flames. Level 1: two low black-stone cottages and short charred fence. Level 2: two cottages inside a simple palisade. Level 3: compact basalt wall, two houses and a brazier. Level 4: four corner towers, two houses and thin lava cracks. Level 5: medium infernal keep, two low buildings and compact perimeter. Level 6: slightly larger fortress keep, four low buildings, centered gate and modest flames. Keep flames below rooftops and within the village silhouette; broad shapes, few windows and limited detail. No people, demons, gore, skulls, text, logos, flags, scenery or watermark. True transparent background.

La génération brute est `ville-enfer-generation.png`. La planche normalisée mesure 1200 × 680. Découpe reproductible : `php scripts/process-ville-enfer-sheet.php` depuis la racine du projet.
