# Planche des villages napoléoniens

Mode : imagegen intégré, fond transparent. La planche romaine du projet a servi de référence pour la perspective et la disposition seulement.

Prompt final :

> Create a new transparent game sprite sheet for six Napoleonic-era French villages, using the Roman sheet only as a reference for the 3-column by 2-row layout, compact isometric 3/4 perspective, visual scale, crisp hand-painted game-art style, and clear growth between levels. Do not reuse Roman architecture. Six separate freestanding villages in reading order, with wide transparent gutters. Historical French First Empire details: pale limestone or cream plaster walls, dark slate roofs, restrained imperial blue and red pennants, cobbled yards, fences, palisades, then angular Vauban-style stone ramparts and cannons. Level 1: two cottages and fence. Level 2: cottages, garrison house and stockade. Level 3: four buildings, gate and fortified post. Level 4: barracks, gate and stone ramparts. Level 5: military town and neoclassical headquarters. Level 6: grand imperial fortified town with colonnaded headquarters and central square. Consistent elevated view and lighting; simple readable silhouettes at tiny map size. No people, horses, text, labels, numbers, logos, watermarks or scenery outside each base. True transparent background.

Découpe reproductible : `php scripts/process-napoleon-sheet.php` depuis la racine du projet.
