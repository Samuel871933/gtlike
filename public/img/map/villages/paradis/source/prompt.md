# Planche « Paradis »

Mode : imagegen intégré, fond transparent. **Version retenue : première génération**, avec dômes dorés, jardins et nuages. La planche romaine sert de référence pour la perspective et la progression des tailles ; le script recale les six silhouettes sur ses empreintes.

Prompt retenu :

> Create six transparent upgrade-level map sprites for a paradise / heavenly city. Use the Roman village sheet as a strict 3-by-2 composition template: same compact footprints, elevated isometric 3/4 camera, roof orientations, centered front entrance, bottom baseline, relative sizes, broad transparent gutters and readable game-icon silhouettes. Replace Roman architecture with warm ivory and white marble, pale gold roofs and trim, turquoise pools, planted gardens and small white clouds at the base. Grow from two low cottages at level 1 to a three-tier celestial palace, four low houses and a compact wall at level 6. Keep the clouds below and within each village silhouette, with enough contrast to read on grass. No people, angels, wings, religious symbols, text, logos, flags or opaque sky. True transparent background.

La génération brute est `paradis-generation.png`. La planche normalisée mesure 1200 × 680. Découpe reproductible : `php scripts/process-paradis-sheet.php` depuis la racine du projet.
