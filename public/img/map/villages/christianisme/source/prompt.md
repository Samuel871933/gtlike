# Planche « Christianisme »

Générée avec l’outil image intégré, en prenant les planches Grèce antique et Rome antique comme références de style, de perspective et d’espacement.

> Create one coherent 3 columns by 2 rows sprite sheet of six separate medieval Christian European villages on a transparent background. Use a clear isometric three-quarter view with front gates at the bottom, compact village footprints and detailed hand-painted strategy-game art. Progress from two cottages and a chapel, to a palisaded parish, to a stone-walled church village, an abbey town, a larger twin-tower church, and finally a cathedral city with a full stone enceinte. Use warm ivory limestone, terracotta and slate roofs, visible Latin crosses, and increasing size and architectural complexity. Keep the perspective, spacing, simplicity and dimensions consistent with the reference village sprite sheets. No people, lettering, numbers, interface, watermark, ground plane or overlapping sprites.

La planche PNG transparente est découpée par `scripts/process-christianisme-sheet.php` en six fichiers `level-N.png` aux dimensions des autres designs.
