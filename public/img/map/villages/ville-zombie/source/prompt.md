# Planche « Ville apocalypse zombie »

Mode : imagegen intégré, fond transparent. La planche romaine sert de gabarit pour la perspective, les six positions et la progression des tailles.

Prompt final :

> Create six transparent upgrade-level map sprites for a zombie-apocalypse modern city. Use the Roman village sheet as a strict 3-by-2 composition template: same compact footprints, elevated isometric 3/4 camera, centered front entrance, bottom baseline, relative sizes, broad transparent gutters and readable simple game-icon silhouettes. Replace Roman buildings with weathered modern concrete houses and low apartment blocks, boarded windows, rusty roofs, moss and restrained red warnings. Level 1: two abandoned houses and broken fence. Level 2: two houses and barricade. Level 3: three ruined buildings and concrete wall. Level 4: fortified street block with low guard posts. Level 5: one modest damaged apartment block and lower houses. Level 6: a slightly taller ruined apartment block with two smaller blocks and compact fortified perimeter. Convey apocalypse through cracks, boards and sparse vines, not dense debris. No zombies, people, gore, fire, smoke, vehicles, text, logos or background skyline. True transparent background.

La génération brute est `ville-zombie-generation.png`. La planche normalisée mesure 1200 × 680. Découpe reproductible : `php scripts/process-ville-zombie-sheet.php` depuis la racine du projet.
